Who finishes uni in Australia?

Starting a degree is the easy part. Whether you finish depends on what you study, where you study and how you study.

1. The big picture

62%

of students who started a bachelor degree in 2017 had finished six years later. Overseas students finished at 79%.

Share finishing within six years, by start year

2. What you study

Completion rates differ a lot between fields of study. Every field finished less often for 2017 starters than for 2005 starters, and Education fell the furthest, by about 19 percentage points.

4. Who you are

Students from metropolitan areas finished at 63%, regional students at 57% and remote students at 53%. First Nations students finished at 40%, against 62% for non-Indigenous students.

5. How you study

Students who study online or by distance finish far less often (39%) than those studying on campus (66%).
